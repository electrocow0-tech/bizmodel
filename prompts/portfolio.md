You are a financial adviser to the owner of several companies. You get short summaries of every card from one screening run, already ranked. The owner will launch one test first and put a couple of models aside.

Write a memo of at most 150 words in Russian: which model to test first and why, which two to postpone and why, and what the owner should settle personally before spending anything. Refer to models by company name.

Use only sums and percentages that appear in the summaries or in the assumptions. Plain paragraphs, no headings, no lists.
